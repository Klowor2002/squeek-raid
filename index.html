Every 25 m the real wheel turns earns you +1 Energy. Perfect = the centre ±4% of the bar (±7% while Squeektoshi runs). Chain Perfects to build a combo — damage grows up to ×2, and Perfects landed at combo ×3 or higher bank Surge charges that amplify the hamster's next settlement strike.

How to play
Strike: press ATTACK or Space as the white marker crosses the bar. Gold centre = Perfect (×3 damage, or a ×5 mega-crit while the hamster runs). Green = a solid hit. Edges = a graze.
Energy: each attack costs Energy (2 during the Fridge Raider's Deep Freeze). Energy refills from the real distance Squeektoshi runs — 25 m per point — or slowly via Focus.
Combos & Surge: consecutive Perfects build your combo multiplier. At combo ×3+, every Perfect also banks a Surge charge (max 5). When a round settles on-chain, Squeektoshi strikes the boss — and your banked Surge makes that strike hit +20% harder per charge.
Boss mechanics: at 66% and 33% HP every boss unleashes its signature move for 12 seconds — guards that only Perfects pierce, dust shields, a frozen or reversing marker, a faster bar, a narrower Perfect window. Watch the banner.
Enrage: dawdle and the boss enrages when its round budget runs out: it heals and armours up. Bosses remember every defeat — check the scar and the memory line under its name.
Progression: daily quests and Wheel Pass tiers pay Raid Tokens. Spend them in the Upgrade Tree. Your companion changes how you play — pick one under the arena.

      About this build — how SQUEEK RAID uses Squeekchain

      SQUEEK RAID is not a dashboard with a button glued on. The chain is the game's engine: a real hamster's real running is the energy economy, real on-chain settlements are the raid's heavy artillery, and real rounds are the clock everything is paced against. Everything below runs from Squeekchain's public API — no keys, no wallet, no backend, no tracking.

      EndpointPolledWhat it drives in the game
      GET /v1/status10 sWheel animation speed (real RPM), Energy from real distance deltas, the running buff, live round number, daily-quest distance & running-damage tracking, Wheel Pass witnessed metres.
      GET /v1/settlements?range=24h60 sSettlement detection: when settledThroughRound advances, Squeektoshi strikes for workAdded × 2 damage (amplified by your banked Surge), with the settlement tx linked in the raid log. Also the next-settlement countdown and settlement-witness quests.
      GET /v1/settlements?range=7d5 minThe Settlement Timeline below the arena: the last settlements with rounds covered, work, blocks and Etherscan links, plus a work-per-settlement chart.
      GET /v1/measurement/stats?range=30d5 minThe Hamster Almanac: 30 days of nightly distance, career totals, best day, and the wheel's peak running window.
      GET /v1/economy/state5 minThe SqueekFi snapshot: live SQK supply vs. cap from the token contract the API reports.

      Running-detection rule: Squeektoshi counts as RUNNING iff rpmMilli > 0 or speedMmPerSecond > 0. The API's coreStatus is never trusted alone — it can report IDLE while the wheel is physically turning.

      Anti-fake rules the game holds itself to: (1) the first settlement seen after loading only sets a baseline — no retroactive damage for rounds you weren't here for; (2) distance is counted only from live deltas, and if the API's counter epoch changes, the baseline resets instead of crediting a teleporting hamster; (3) stale or offline data is badged as such, never dressed up as live; (4) every API number arrives as a string and is parsed defensively — a malformed field degrades one readout, never the game.

      Design notes: bosses were given mechanics, memory and grudges (enrage timers) so the raid is a game first and a data demo second; quests and the Wheel Pass only count things you genuinely witnessed, so progress can't be farmed by leaving a tab open on a dead wheel — the wheel has to actually turn.

      Streamers: open this page with ?overlay=1 for a compact overlay view (wheel, boss HP, RPM, round, settlement countdown) built for an OBS browser source. Collectors: the "Export badge (SVG)" button in the save card generates a dynamic raider badge — an SVG portrait of your career (title, level, bosses felled, best combo, current week) with ERC-721-style metadata embedded, ready if the wheel ever gets a mint.
