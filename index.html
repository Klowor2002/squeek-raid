    How it works: SQUEEK RAID reads Squeekchain's public live API. Your attacks are a timing game; your Energy refills from the real distance Squeektoshi runs; and every time a round settles on-chain, Squeektoshi himself smacks the boss for damage based on the work in that settlement.
    Rules: points only — no wallet connection, no transactions, no money, no betting. Progress lives in your browser's local storage only; nothing is tracked or sent anywhere.
